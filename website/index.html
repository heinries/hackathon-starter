THE EVERYDAY INFORMATION GAP

The small things
don’t always make
the map.

A pin tells you where a place is.
A neighbour can add what it’s like.

Details about surfaces, lighting and shared spaces can be scattered, missing or out of date. Anole imagines a common language across public and privately operated places, with each observation’s source, date and uncertainty kept in view.
