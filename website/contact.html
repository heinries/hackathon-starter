Non-emergency city requests

Houston 311 is the official starting point for non-emergency city service requests. Use its website to find the appropriate reporting channel for a real issue.

Visit official Houston 311 ↗

Houston 311 already provides a public service-request map ↗. Anole’s proposed role is to bring community observations and follow-up actions together across public and privately operated places.

You’ll leave this prototype. Anole does not submit to 311, check case status or use a 311 write API.

About this demo

All four observations, locations, conditions and dates are fictional. The map is an illustration, not a navigation tool. No opening hours or accessible routes are confirmed.

Capture fields exist only in your browser page. Nothing is saved or sent, and there is no monitored contact form.
