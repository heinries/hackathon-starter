FIELD NOTES / HOUSTON HACKATHON 2026

One shared language.
Many ways to care.

Anole is a concept for community-created observations about public and privately operated places and infrastructure. The vision is to help neighbours notice and describe local conditions, then follow up on community action: what was noticed, what people tried and what worked. Follow-up records are a future capability; this site does not store observations.

Anole’s proposed distinction is one shared neighbourhood view across place types, community observations, follow-up actions and solutions. A future observation could describe seating someone saw at a privately operated mall, with its date and a community-observed, unverified label. Any cooling, access or opening-hours claim would need its own source or direct observation.

Houston 311 already maps public service requests. For city-maintained problems, use official 311 reporting or its public request map. Anole has not submitted any requests.

Local knowledge,
with its limits visible.

An observation is a moment in time. Anole’s proposed interface keeps the condition, date and verification status together, so uncertainty stays visible.

Meet Bole, Anole’s fictional brown-anole mascot. Bole’s upward-curled tail points out something worth noticing: a community observation, never an official warning or a verified city report.

A NOTE ON WHAT WORKS TODAY

A small, honest starting point.

In this prototype

Explore shows fictional sample observations on an interactive basemap, with working category filters. The pins are arbitrary demo positions, not verified places.

Capture is an unsaved, on-page preview. You can edit fields and review a note; nothing is uploaded, stored or submitted.

Future possibilities

Follow-up records of community action, photo upload, GPS, community submissions, database storage, moderation and 311 integration are possibilities for future development. None are implemented in this website.

Today, Anole only links to official Houston 311 reporting and its public request map. It does not send reports or check case status.

An independent community concept for Houston, not an official City of Houston service.
